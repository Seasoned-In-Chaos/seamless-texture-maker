# Corona Renderer

SEAMS does not currently include a Corona-specific export preset. Export the individual maps from [PBR Export](/pages/export/pbr-export) and wire them into a CoronaPhysicalMtl in your 3ds Max or Cinema 4D scene.

<Tip>
Corona expects OpenGL-convention (Y+) normal maps — the default in Material Lab's Normal controls.
</Tip>
