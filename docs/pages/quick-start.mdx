# Quick Start

## 1. Open an Image

Press `Ctrl+O`, drag an image onto the window, or double-click the empty canvas. SEAMS opens PNG, JPEG, TIFF, TGA, EXR and PSD, from 64 × 64 to 8192 × 8192 pixels, in 8-bit, 16-bit and float.

## 2. Crop (Optional)

Press `C` for the Crop tool, or `Shift+C` for a perspective crop. Draw, move, resize or turn the frame, then press `Enter`. See [Crop, Retouch and Check](/pages/tools).

## 3. Delight (Optional)

Press `1`. Choose a preset (Soft, Balanced or Aggressive) or move the sliders, then click **Apply delight**. All sliders start at 0, so nothing changes until you move one.

<Note>
Applying delight replaces the base color. Undo with `Ctrl+Z`, up to 20 steps.
</Note>

## 4. Make Seamless

Press `2`. Pick a method and adjust it; the canvas updates as you drag.

<Tip>
**Offset cross-fade** is fast and predictable. **Mirror tiling** keeps every detail but is visibly symmetric. **Overlap blend** suits structured materials and fabrics. **Splat synthesis** breaks up repetition in organic materials.
</Tip>

Switch the view bar to **Tiled** and turn on the tiling check (`Ctrl+Shift+T`) to see any seam or repeat that is left. To paint out what remains, click **Use result as base** and retouch with `J`.

## 5. Generate Material Maps

Press `3`. The maps come from the seamless base color. Adjust each map's settings, and click a thumbnail under the canvas to look at it.

## 6. Export

- `Ctrl+S` saves the base color. Whether that makes a new file or replaces the original is set by the **Replace original file** checkbox above the export button.
- `Ctrl+E` exports whatever is selected in the strip under the canvas: the base color or one of the maps.
