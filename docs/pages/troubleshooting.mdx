# Troubleshooting

## The Window Is Off-Screen or the Wrong Size

**Fix**: close SEAMS and delete `%APPDATA%\SeamlessTextureMaker\settings.json`. The window then opens at its default size.

---

## Previews Are Slow, or the GPU Is Not Used

SEAMS draws live previews on the GPU when it can (DirectX 12, Vulkan or Metal) and on the CPU otherwise. **Help › About** says which. Common reasons for the CPU:

- The graphics driver is out of date. Update it.
- The image is larger than the GPU's texture limit.
- The method is **Splat synthesis**, or the stage is **Delight**: both are CPU-only.
- `SEAMS_CPU_PREVIEW=1` is set.

If the GPU stops responding, SEAMS says so in the status bar and uses the CPU for the rest of the session.

---

## Splat Results Look Repetitive

- Raise **Wobble** and **Random rotation** for more variety.
- Try a different **Material preset**, or the **Shuffle** button for a new layout.
- Use a smaller **Patch size**.

---

## A Seam Is Still Visible

1. Turn on the tiling check (`Ctrl+Shift+T`) and the **Tiled** view to see where.
2. Try another seamless method, or a larger overlap or fade.
3. For what is left, click **Use result as base** in the Seamless stage and paint it out with the Retouch tool (`J`) with **Wrap around edges** on.

---

## Normal Map Looks Wrong

- **OpenGL renderers** (Blender, Corona): use the **OpenGL (Y+)** format.
- **DirectX renderers** (Unreal, V-Ray): use the **DirectX (Y-)** format.
- If bumps look like dents, turn on **Invert height**.

---

## Nothing Happens When I Drag

A plain drag does not move the view, as in Photoshop. Pan with the pan tool (`H`), by holding `Space`, or with the middle mouse button. With the Crop or Retouch tool open, a drag works on the image instead.

---

## Where Are the Logs?

`%APPDATA%\SeamlessTextureMaker\logs\seams.log`. Include it when you report a problem.
